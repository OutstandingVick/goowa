The State of Stablecoin Reserves, 2024

Stablecoins are tokens designed to hold a steady value against a reference asset, most often the U.S. dollar. Issuers say they keep reserve assets so that each token can be redeemed at face value.

Reserve composition differs widely between issuers. Some publish monthly attestation reports from accounting firms, while others disclose far less about what backs their tokens.
